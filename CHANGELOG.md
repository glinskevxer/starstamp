# Changes

## 1.0.0

The first version the shop runs on its own book rather than on a copy.

Everything a requalification facility does between a cylinder arriving and
leaving: receiving lots, visual inspection, the four requalification methods
and the criteria each one produces, calibration and the validity it decides,
stars and plus ratings, intervals, filling decisions, repairs, re-marking,
condemnation and disposal, and the letters that go with all of it.

Alongside that: lots and bookings against the bench's real capacity, a price
book and per account agreements, invoices, storage charges, statements and
aging, and thirty reports over the top of the lot.

Records of test are append only. A record that turns out to rest on something
that moved is reversed and replaced, never edited, whether that is a mistyped
reading or a calibration certificate the calibration house restated two years
later.

Arithmetic is exact throughout: expansion in tenths of a cubic centimetre,
pressure in whole psi, money in cents, ratios as fractions.

Requires Python 3.12 or 3.13, and click. Everything else is the standard
library.
